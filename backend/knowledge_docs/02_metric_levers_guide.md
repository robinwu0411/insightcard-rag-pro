# Metric Levers Guide

This document maps each key vendor performance metric to its controllable levers, helping identify root causes and actionable recommendations.

## Net PPM (Net Profit Per Million)
**Definition**: Profitability metric measuring net profit after COGS, trade terms, and deductions per million in shipped revenue.

**Key Levers**:
- **Cost Price Optimization**: Negotiate with manufacturer, reduce packaging costs, optimize logistics
- **Trade Terms**: Negotiate improved trade terms (co-op, freight, damage allowances)
- **Promotion Strategy**: Shift from deep discounting to targeted promotions on high-margin ASINs
- **Mix Shift**: Promote higher-margin ASINs in the catalog
- **Damage Reduction**: Improve packaging to reduce damage-related deductions
- **Returns Reduction**: Improve product quality and detail page accuracy to reduce returns

**Warning Signs**:
- Net PPM declining while Revenue growing: margin compression, likely from discounting or cost increases
- Net PPM declining with stable Revenue: trade term erosion or damage/returns increase
- Net PPM below goal by >10%: immediate pricing review required

## Revenue (Shipped Revenue)
**Definition**: Total revenue from products shipped to Amazon fulfillment centers.

**Key Levers**:
- **In-Stock Rate**: Every 1% drop in in-stock rate correlates to ~0.7% revenue loss
- **Advertising Spend**: Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display
- **Conversion Rate (CR)**: Detail page quality, pricing, reviews
- **Glance Views**: Search ranking, advertising, traffic drivers
- **ASIN Count**: New ASIN launches, variation expansion
- **Promotion Participation**: Lightning Deals, Prime Exclusive Discounts, Coupons

**Warning Signs**:
- Revenue declining with stable Glance Views: CR dropping, check pricing and reviews
- Revenue declining with stable CR: Glance Views dropping, check advertising and search ranking
- Revenue below goal: prioritize in-stock recovery and advertising optimization

## Conversion Rate (CR)
**Definition**: Percentage of detail page views that result in a purchase.

**Key Levers**:
- **Price Competitiveness**: Compare to Buy Box price and competitor pricing
- **Detail Page Quality**: Images (main + lifestyle), bullet points, A+ Content
- **Review Count and Rating**: Target minimum 15 reviews with 4.0+ average rating
- **Availability**: In-stock status directly impacts CR
- **Fast Shipping**: Prime badge and fast delivery promises

**Warning Signs**:
- CR below 5%: likely pricing or review issue
- CR declining week-over-week: check for recent price increases or review changes
- CR varies significantly across ASINs: catalog-level issues, not vendor-level

## Glance Views
**Definition**: Number of times product detail pages are viewed.

**Key Levers**:
- **Advertising**: Sponsored Products (keyword and product targeting), Sponsored Brands
- **Search Ranking**: Optimize for relevant keywords, improve organic ranking
- **Brand Store**: Drive traffic through brand store
- **External Traffic**: Social media, influencer partnerships, off-Amazon campaigns
- **Promotions**: Deals and coupons increase visibility

**Warning Signs**:
- Glance Views declining: check advertising budget and bid changes
- Glance Views stable but Revenue declining: CR issue
- Low Glance Views on high-margin ASINs: reallocate advertising budget

## In-Stock Rate
**Definition**: Percentage of time ASINs are available for purchase.

**Key Levers**:
- **Forecast Accuracy**: Improve demand forecasting, account for seasonality
- **Lead Time**: Reduce manufacturing and shipping lead times
- **Order Frequency**: Increase purchase order frequency to reduce stock-out risk
- **Safety Stock**: Maintain safety stock for top-performing ASINs
- **Vendor Central Compliance**: Respond to purchase orders within 24 hours

**Warning Signs**:
- In-stock rate below 95%: immediate supply chain review
- In-stock rate below 90%: critical, revenue impact significant
- Recurring stock-outs on same ASIN: forecast or lead time issue

## Inventory Health
**Definition**: Composite metric tracking excess inventory, sell-through, and stranded inventory.

**Key Levers**:
- **Excess Inventory**: Reduce over-ordering, improve forecast accuracy
- **Sell-Through Rate**: Target 80%+ sell-through for healthy ASINs
- **Stranded Inventory**: Fix listing issues to make ASINs available
- **Aging Inventory**: Create removal orders for 365+ day inventory
- **Fees Optimization**: Reduce long-term storage fees through proactive management

**Warning Signs**:
- Excess inventory growing: reduce purchase order quantities
- Sell-through declining: check CR and Glance Views
- Stranded inventory >5% of total: urgent listing fix required
