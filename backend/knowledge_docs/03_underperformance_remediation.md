# Underperformance Remediation Playbook

This playbook provides structured action plans when vendor metrics fall below JBP goals. Use the gap percentage and trend to determine urgency and action scope.

## Net PPM Below Goal

### Gap 5-10% (Yellow Alert)
1. Review recent trade term changes - check if new deductions were applied
2. Identify top 5 ASINs with largest PPM decline - check for cost price increases
3. Review damage and returns rate - if increased, investigate packaging quality
4. Action: Schedule cost optimization review with procurement team within 7 days

### Gap 10-20% (Orange Alert)
1. Perform full margin analysis by ASIN - identify loss-making ASINs
2. Review promotion history - check if deep discounting eroded margin
3. Evaluate product mix - calculate PPM by category and identify low-margin categories
4. Action: Negotiate trade terms improvement with Amazon account team within 14 days
5. Action: Pause promotions on ASINs with PPM below threshold

### Gap >20% (Red Alert)
1. Immediate pricing review on all ASINs - identify pricing errors
2. Full cost structure audit - manufacturing, logistics, packaging, trade terms
3. Evaluate strategic options: discontinue loss-making ASINs, renegotiate supplier contracts
4. Action: Submit formal Net PPM recovery plan to BS within 5 business days
5. Action: Schedule joint review with BS, procurement, and finance within 10 days

## Revenue Below Goal

### Gap 5-10% (Yellow Alert)
1. Check in-stock rate - if below 95%, prioritize supply recovery for top ASINs
2. Review advertising campaign performance - identify campaigns with low ROAS
3. Check for recent ASIN suppressions or listing issues
4. Action: Increase advertising budget by 10-15% on top-performing ASINs

### Gap 10-20% (Orange Alert)
1. Conduct full ASIN-level revenue analysis - identify declining ASINs
2. Review competitive landscape - check for new competitors or price undercutting
3. Evaluate promotion calendar - ensure participation in upcoming deals events
4. Action: Launch recovery promotion (coupon or Prime Exclusive Discount) on top 20 ASINs
5. Action: Submit supply recovery plan for out-of-stock ASINs within 7 days

### Gap >20% (Red Alert)
1. Full revenue diagnostic: decompose into Glance Views x CR x Price
2. Identify whether issue is traffic (Glance Views), conversion (CR), or both
3. Review catalog health - check for mass ASIN suppressions
4. Action: Emergency advertising budget increase of 25-30% with focus on branded keywords
5. Action: Schedule emergency joint review with BS within 48 hours
6. Action: Submit 90-day revenue recovery plan within 5 business days

## Conversion Rate Below Goal

### Gap 10-20% (Yellow Alert)
1. Review Buy Box win rate - check if losing Buy Box to third-party sellers
2. Audit detail page quality - images, bullet points, A+ Content completeness
3. Check review rating trend - identify ASINs with rating drops
4. Action: Enroll underperforming ASINs in Vine program for review generation

### Gap 20-35% (Orange Alert)
1. Competitive pricing analysis - compare top 20 ASINs to Buy Box price and competitors
2. Detail page audit - ensure all ASINs have 7+ images and optimized bullets
3. Review for recent negative reviews - identify root cause (quality, expectation mismatch)
4. Action: Price adjustment to match or beat Buy Box on top 10 ASINs within 7 days
5. Action: Submit detail page optimization plan for top 50 ASINs within 14 days

### Gap >35% (Red Alert)
1. Full catalog audit - identify systematic issues (pricing, content, reviews)
2. Check for policy violations or suppressions
3. Evaluate product quality - high return rate indicates quality issues
4. Action: Immediate price competitiveness review on all ASINs
5. Action: Product quality investigation for ASINs with return rate >15%
6. Action: Submit comprehensive CR recovery plan within 5 business days

## In-Stock Rate Below Goal

### Gap 2-5% (Yellow Alert)
1. Identify specific out-of-stock ASINs and duration
2. Check purchase order status - are there pending POs not shipped?
3. Review lead time accuracy - compare actual vs expected lead times
4. Action: Ship pending POs immediately, confirm next PO delivery dates

### Gap 5-10% (Orange Alert)
1. Full supply chain audit - identify root cause (forecast, lead time, capacity)
2. Prioritize top 20 revenue ASINs for immediate stock recovery
3. Review forecast accuracy - compare vendor forecast to actual Amazon demand
4. Action: Submit supply recovery plan with specific ASIN-level commitments within 7 days
5. Action: Increase safety stock for top 20 ASINs by 20%

### Gap >10% (Red Alert)
1. Emergency supply chain review - identify systemic issues
2. Check for manufacturing disruptions, logistics delays, or vendor capacity issues
3. Evaluate alternative sourcing or expedited shipping options
4. Action: Daily in-stock monitoring for top 50 ASINs
5. Action: Submit comprehensive supply chain recovery plan within 48 hours
6. Action: Schedule emergency meeting with BS and supply chain team within 24 hours
