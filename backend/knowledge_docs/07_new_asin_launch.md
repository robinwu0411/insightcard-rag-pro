# New ASIN Launch Playbook

## Overview
New ASIN launches are a critical growth driver for Amazon vendors. The first 90 days determine long-term success. This playbook covers the end-to-end launch process from preparation to scale.

## Pre-Launch Checklist (T-30 to T-0)

### Product Preparation
1. **Finalize product specifications**: Ensure product meets Amazon compliance requirements
2. **Obtain necessary certifications**: FDA, FCC, UL, or category-specific certifications
3. **Prepare initial inventory**: Minimum 4 weeks of forecasted demand at Amazon FC
4. **Optimize packaging**: Amazon-ready packaging (ISTN-6 certified, barcode on outer carton)

### Catalog Setup
1. **Create ASIN**: Submit product information through Vendor Central or AMS catalog team
2. **Complete all attributes**: Title, brand, manufacturer, model number, category, keywords
3. **Upload high-quality images**: Minimum 7 images (main, lifestyle, infographic, packaging)
4. **Write optimized bullet points**: 5 bullets, each highlighting a key benefit with keywords
5. **Create A+ Content**: Enhanced brand content for premium positioning
6. **Set competitive launch price**: Research Buy Box price for similar products

### Launch Readiness
1. **Inventory positioned**: Confirm inventory received at Amazon FC before launch
2. **Advertising campaigns prepared**: Auto and manual Sponsored Products campaigns ready
3. **Vine enrollment**: Enroll in Amazon Vine program for early reviews
4. **Brand Store update**: Add new ASIN to Brand Store
5. **Promotion ready**: Coupon or Prime Exclusive Discount prepared for launch week

## Launch Phase (Day 1-30)

### Week 1: Visibility Push
1. **Launch advertising**: Start with auto-targeting Sponsored Products at 150% of target budget
2. **Enable coupon**: 10-15% off coupon for first 2 weeks to drive initial sales
3. **Monitor listing health**: Check for suppressions, pricing errors, availability issues daily
4. **Track metrics**: Glance Views, CR, advertising ROAS, units sold

### Week 2-3: Optimization
1. **Review search term report**: Identify converting keywords from auto campaign
2. **Create manual campaigns**: Move high-performing keywords to exact match campaigns
3. **Adjust bids**: Optimize CPC based on conversion data
4. **Check Vine reviews**: Monitor incoming reviews, respond to any negative feedback
5. **Adjust pricing**: If CR is low, consider price adjustment or coupon increase

### Week 4: Assessment
1. **Performance review**: Compare actual vs forecast for Glance Views, CR, Revenue
2. **Advertising optimization**: Pause low-performing keywords, increase budget on winners
3. **Review detail page**: Update images or bullets based on customer questions/feedback
4. **Plan next month**: Adjust strategy based on performance data

## Growth Phase (Day 31-90)

### Month 2: Scale
1. **Expand advertising**: Add Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display campaigns
2. **Keyword expansion**: Add broad and phrase match keywords for discovery
3. **Competitor targeting**: Launch product targeting campaigns against competitor ASINs
4. **Promotion participation**: Apply for Lightning Deals or 7-day deals
5. **Review generation**: Continue Vine program, follow up on early customer reviews

### Month 3: Stabilize
1. **ROAS optimization**: Shift budget from low-ROAS to high-ROAS campaigns
2. **Organic ranking**: Monitor and optimize for organic search ranking on key keywords
3. **Inventory planning**: Adjust inventory based on actual sell-through data
4. **Pricing review**: Evaluate pricing strategy, adjust if needed for long-term profitability
5. **90-day review**: Comprehensive performance review with BS

## Success Metrics for New ASINs

### Week 1 Targets
- Glance Views: > 500 per ASIN
- CR: > 3% (new ASIN without reviews)
- Advertising ROAS: > 1.5x (investment phase)
- Units sold: > 20 per ASIN

### Month 1 Targets
- Glance Views: > 2,000 per ASIN
- CR: > 5% (with reviews building up)
- Advertising ROAS: > 2.5x
- Reviews: > 5 reviews (via Vine)
- Revenue: > $5,000 per ASIN

### Month 3 Targets
- Glance Views: > 5,000 per ASIN
- CR: > 7% (comparable to catalog average)
- Advertising ROAS: > 3.5x
- Reviews: > 15 reviews with 4.0+ rating
- Revenue: > $15,000 per ASIN
- Organic sales: > 40% of total (reducing advertising dependency)

## Common New ASIN Issues and Solutions

### Low Glance Views (< 500 in Week 1)
- **Cause**: Insufficient advertising, poor keyword targeting, or listing suppression
- **Solution**: Increase advertising budget, check listing health, expand keyword targeting

### Low CR (< 3%)
- **Cause**: Price too high, poor detail page quality, no reviews
- **Solution**: Price adjustment, improve images and bullets, enroll in Vine, add coupon

### Low Advertising ROAS (< 1x)
- **Cause**: Irrelevant keywords, low CR, high CPC
- **Solution**: Add negative keywords, shift to exact match, improve detail page quality

### Out of Stock
- **Cause**: Underestimated demand or supply chain delay
- **Solution**: Expedite inventory, increase safety stock, adjust advertising to match availability

## New ASIN Contribution to JBP Goals
- Each successful new ASIN should contribute 5-15% of incremental annual revenue
- Target: Launch 2-4 new ASINs per quarter for Tier 1 vendors
- Track new ASIN revenue contribution as percentage of total revenue (target > 10%)
- Include new ASIN pipeline in JBP planning with projected launch dates and revenue impact
