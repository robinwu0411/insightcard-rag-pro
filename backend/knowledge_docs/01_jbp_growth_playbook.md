# JBP Growth Playbook

## Overview
The Joint Business Plan (JBP) is the foundational growth framework for Amazon vendors. It aligns vendor and Amazon on shared goals, input metrics, and growth actions across a 12-month planning cycle. The JBP focuses on output metrics (Revenue, Net PPM) driven by input actions (advertising, inventory management, new ASIN launches, pricing optimization).

## Growth Drivers (Flywheel)
The Amazon vendor flywheel consists of 5 core growth drivers:
1. **Selection Expansion** - New ASIN launches, variation additions, catalog enrichment
2. **Traffic Generation** - Advertising, organic search optimization, brand store
3. **Conversion Optimization** - Detail page quality, pricing competitiveness, reviews
4. **Supply Chain Reliability** - In-stock rate, fill rate, lead time optimization
5. **Profitability Management** - Cost optimization, trade terms, pricing strategy

## Growth Action Taxonomy
### Tier 1 Actions (High Impact, 0-30 days)
- Fix out-of-stock ASINs immediately
- Adjust advertising bids for underperforming campaigns
- Correct pricing errors (cost-based)
- Resolve detail page issues (images, bullets, descriptions)

### Tier 2 Actions (Medium Impact, 30-90 days)
- Launch new variations of top-performing ASINs
- Optimize advertising campaign structure (move from auto to manual targeting)
- Implement Vine program for new ASINs to build reviews
- Negotiate improved trade terms with Amazon

### Tier 3 Actions (Strategic, 90-180 days)
- Develop entirely new ASINs in growth categories
- Build Brand Store and A+ Content
- Implement Subscribe & Save program
- Expand to additional Amazon marketplaces

## Metric Goal Setting
Goals should be set using a combination of:
- Historical performance (trailing 13-week average as baseline)
- Category growth rate (apply category CAGR to baseline)
- Vendor-specific growth actions planned (additive lift estimate)
- Amazon strategic priorities (JBP shared goals)

## When to Escalate
- Metric gap exceeds 15% for 2 consecutive weeks: escalate to BS
- Metric gap exceeds 25%: require formal recovery plan within 5 business days
- Multiple metrics simultaneously below goal: schedule joint review with BS and vendor

## Vendor Tier Segmentation
- **Tier 1 (Strategic)**: >$10M annual revenue. Full BS support, quarterly business reviews, custom growth plans
- **Tier 2 (Growth)**: $1M-$10M annual revenue. Shared BS support, monthly check-ins, standardized growth playbooks
- **Tier 3 (Emerging)**: <$1M annual revenue. Self-service tools, automated recommendations, BS escalation only for critical issues
