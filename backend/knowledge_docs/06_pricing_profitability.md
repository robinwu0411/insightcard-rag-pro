# Pricing and Profitability Guide

## Overview
Net PPM (Net Profit Per Million) is the core profitability metric for Amazon vendors. This guide covers pricing strategy, cost optimization, and margin management to maintain healthy profitability.

## Pricing Strategy Framework

### Cost-Plus Pricing
- Calculate fully loaded cost: manufacturing + packaging + logistics + trade terms + Amazon fees
- Apply target margin: Net PPM goal + buffer (typically 5-10% above goal)
- Review quarterly: Adjust for cost changes, trade term changes, fee changes
- Use for: New ASIN pricing, cost-based price corrections

### Competitive Pricing
- Monitor Buy Box price for top 20 ASINs daily
- Target: Price within 5% of Buy Box price for established ASINs
- Use Amazon Brand Analytics Price Analysis tool for competitive insights
- Adjust pricing when Buy Box price moves by more than 5%

### Value-Based Pricing
- Price based on customer perceived value for differentiated products
- Use A+ Content and Brand Store to communicate value
- Monitor review sentiment for pricing feedback signals
- Use for: Premium products, exclusive selection, innovative features

## Net PPM Optimization Levers

### Cost Price Reduction
1. **Manufacturing optimization**: Renegotiate with suppliers, optimize production runs
2. **Packaging optimization**: Reduce packaging size and weight (also reduces shipping and damage)
3. **Logistics optimization**: Optimize freight terms, consider FOB vs DDP
4. **Volume rebates**: Negotiate volume-based rebates with suppliers
5. **Annual cost review**: Conduct comprehensive cost audit annually

### Trade Terms Optimization
1. **Co-op advertising**: Negotiate co-op terms to offset advertising costs
2. **Freight allowance**: Negotiate freight terms (prepaid vs collect)
3. **Damage allowance**: Negotiate damage allowance to cover expected damage rate
4. **Payment terms**: Negotiate payment terms (Net 30, Net 60)
5. **Annual trade term review**: Review and renegotiate annually with Amazon

### Promotion Margin Management
1. **Calculate promotion PPM**: Net PPM after promotion discount and incremental costs
2. **Target promotion ROAS**: Ensure promoted ASINs generate sufficient volume to offset margin loss
3. **Avoid chronic discounting**: Deep discounts erode price perception and long-term margin
4. **Use targeted promotions**: Coupon and Prime Exclusive Discount instead of blanket price reductions
5. **Promotion calendar**: Plan promotions to align with seasonal demand peaks

### Mix Shift Strategy
1. **Identify high-margin ASINs**: Calculate PPM by ASIN, identify top quartile
2. **Increase visibility**: Allocate advertising budget to high-margin ASINs
3. **Promote high-margin products**: Feature in Brand Store, Sponsored Brands
4. **Deprioritize low-margin ASINs**: Reduce advertising and promotion investment
5. **New ASIN focus**: Launch new ASINs in high-margin categories

## When Net PPM is Below Goal

### Diagnostic Framework
1. **Decompose the gap**: Is it cost increase, trade term change, discounting, or mix shift?
2. **ASIN-level analysis**: Calculate PPM delta by ASIN vs previous period
3. **Category analysis**: Calculate PPM delta by category
4. **Promotion impact**: Calculate margin impact of recent promotions

### Action Plan by Root Cause

#### Cost Increase
1. Identify specific cost components that increased (manufacturing, logistics, packaging)
2. Negotiate cost reduction with suppliers within 30 days
3. Evaluate alternative suppliers or sourcing options
4. If cost increase is permanent, adjust list price to maintain margin

#### Trade Term Erosion
1. Review recent trade term changes in Amazon Vendor Central
2. Identify new deductions or increased rates
3. Negotiate trade terms improvement with Amazon account team
4. Document impact and submit formal trade term review request

#### Excessive Discounting
1. Review promotion history for deep discount events
2. Calculate margin impact of each promotion
3. Shift from price reductions to coupons and targeted promotions
4. Implement promotion approval workflow with margin threshold checks

#### Mix Shift to Low-Margin Products
1. Identify ASINs with revenue growth but low PPM
2. Review advertising allocation - is spend driving low-margin ASIN sales?
3. Reallocate advertising budget to high-margin ASINs
4. Review new ASIN pipeline - ensure focus on high-margin categories

## Pricing Change Workflow
1. **Analysis**: Calculate current PPM, target PPM, required price adjustment
2. **Approval**: Submit price change request through Vendor Central
3. **Implementation**: Price change effective on agreed date
4. **Monitoring**: Track PPM impact for 4 weeks post-change
5. **Adjustment**: Fine-tune if PPM improvement is insufficient

## Profitability by Vendor Tier
- **Tier 1 (Strategic)**: Target Net PPM > 300, quarterly margin reviews, custom pricing strategy
- **Tier 2 (Growth)**: Target Net PPM > 200, semi-annual margin reviews, standardized pricing framework
- **Tier 3 (Emerging)**: Target Net PPM > 150, annual margin reviews, self-service pricing tools
