# Advertising Optimization Guide

## Overview
Advertising is a primary lever for driving Glance Views, Revenue, and brand awareness. This guide covers campaign optimization, budget allocation, and ROAS improvement strategies for Amazon vendors.

## Campaign Types and Use Cases

### Sponsored Products
- **Best for**: Direct revenue impact, keyword targeting, product visibility
- **Targeting options**: Keyword targeting (broad, phrase, exact), product targeting (category, ASIN)
- **ROAS benchmark**: 4-6x for established ASINs, 2-3x acceptable for new ASINs (first 90 days)
- **Optimization tip**: Start with auto-targeting to discover keywords, then move high-performing keywords to manual campaigns

### Sponsored Brands
- **Best for**: Brand awareness, driving traffic to Brand Store, category domination
- **Targeting options**: Keyword targeting
- **ROAS benchmark**: 3-5x
- **Optimization tip**: Use custom headlines highlighting unique value propositions

### Sponsored Display
- **Best for**: Retargeting, audience expansion, competitor conquesting
- **Targeting options**: Product targeting, audience targeting (views, purchases)
- **ROAS benchmark**: 2-4x
- **Optimization tip**: Use for retargeting cart abandoners and cross-selling within brand

## Budget Allocation Strategy

### Tier 1: Core ASINs (Top 20% revenue, established)
- Budget allocation: 60% of total advertising budget
- Strategy: Maintain visibility on branded keywords, defend against competitors
- Target ROAS: 5x+
- Optimization focus: Bid optimization, negative keyword refinement

### Tier 2: Growth ASINs (Mid-tier, growth potential)
- Budget allocation: 25% of total advertising budget
- Strategy: Expand keyword targeting, category conquesting
- Target ROAS: 3-4x
- Optimization focus: Keyword discovery, campaign structure optimization

### Tier 3: New ASINs (Launched within 90 days)
- Budget allocation: 15% of total advertising budget
- Strategy: Aggressive visibility, auto + manual campaigns, review generation
- Target ROAS: 1-2x acceptable (investing in launch)
- Optimization focus: Impression share, click-through rate, first reviews

## When Revenue is Below Goal - Advertising Actions

### Step 1: Diagnose
1. Check total advertising spend vs budget - is spend being utilized?
2. Review campaign-level ROAS - identify underperforming campaigns
3. Check impression share - are ads showing for key keywords?
4. Review search term reports - identify wasted spend on irrelevant terms

### Step 2: Optimize
1. **Increase budget on high-ROAS campaigns**: Reallocate from low-ROAS to high-ROAS
2. **Add negative keywords**: Remove irrelevant search terms consuming budget
3. **Bid adjustment**: Increase bids on top-converting keywords by 15-25%
4. **Launch new campaigns**: Create product targeting campaigns against competitors
5. **Enable placement optimization**: Adjust bid multipliers for Top of Search placement

### Step 3: Expand
1. **Category conquesting**: Target competitor ASINs with product targeting
2. **Broad keyword expansion**: Add relevant broad match keywords
3. **Sponsored Display retargeting**: Retarget users who viewed but didn't purchase
4. **Budget increase**: If ROAS is above target, increase total budget by 15-20%

## ROAS Optimization Playbook

### Low ROAS (< 2x) on Established ASINs
1. Review search term report - add 10+ negative keywords
2. Shift from auto to manual campaigns with exact match
3. Reduce bids by 10-15% on low-converting keywords
4. Check detail page quality - low CR wastes ad spend
5. Consider pausing campaigns for ASINs with CR below 3%

### Declining ROAS Trend
1. Check for increased competition (bid inflation) - adjust bids accordingly
2. Review seasonality - some categories have natural ROAS fluctuations
3. Check for ad fatigue - refresh creative assets for Sponsored Brands
4. Re-evaluate keyword relevance - remove keywords with declining performance

## Advertising for Net PPM Improvement
- Focus advertising spend on high-margin ASINs (calculate margin per ASIN)
- Use Sponsored Products to drive traffic to high-margin ASINs specifically
- Avoid advertising loss-making ASINs (negative ROAS after margin consideration)
- Use placement reports to optimize for profitable placements

## Key Metrics to Monitor
- **TACOS** (Total Advertising Cost of Sales): Target < 10% for healthy vendors
- **ROAS** by campaign type and ASIN
- **Impression Share** on branded keywords: Target > 80%
- **Click-Through Rate (CTR)**: Target > 0.3% for Sponsored Products
- **Advertising Contribution to Revenue**: Target 15-25% of total revenue from ads
